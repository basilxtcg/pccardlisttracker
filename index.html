    Settings and how the score works

    Prices come from TCGplayer in US dollars. CAD prices are converted with the daily exchange rate (from the European Central Bank, through Frankfurter), so treat them as estimates. Data comes through the free TCGdex service, with pokemontcg.io as a backup. Prices refresh about once a day. Near mint cards use the TCGplayer market price. Cards marked "Played OK" use the cheapest listing, since condition doesn't matter for those. Tap the condition tag on any card to switch it.

    The score (0 to 100) mixes three things: how today's price compares with the last 30 days of sales, whether the price is still falling or has levelled off, and where it sits in the price history this app saves each time you open it. A new low gives a big boost. 68 and up is a good time to buy, 45 to 67 is fair, under 45 means wait. It's a rule of thumb, not a guarantee.

    One Piece: the list holds Leaders, Secret Rares, SP, Treasure Rares, and Parallel / Alternate Art / Manga printings for your chosen characters. Prices are TCGplayer market prices (US dollars), saved once a day by the GitHub job, which also builds the price history the score uses. To change the characters, edit data/onepiece-config.json in your GitHub repo. "Remove" hides a One Piece card from your list.

    eBay doesn't share sold prices with apps like this, so the "eBay sold" link opens eBay's sold listings for that card for a quick manual check, with graded slabs (PSA, BGS, CGC and so on) filtered out. All prices in the app are for raw, ungraded cards.
